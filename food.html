Filet Mignon with Rich Balsamic Glaze

Back to Recipes

Description:

This filet mignon recipe features a rich balsamic glaze that enhances the natural flavors of the tender beef. Perfect for a special occasion or a romantic dinner, this dish is sure to impress your guests.

Ingredients:

  • 4 filet mignons
  • 1 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Season the filet mignons with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over high heat.
  4. Cook the filet mignons for 4-5 minutes on each side, or until desired doneness is reached.
  5. In a small saucepan, combine the balsamic vinegar, honey, and garlic.
  6. Simmer the glaze for 10-15 minutes, or until it thickens.
  7. Glaze the cooked filet mignons with the balsamic glaze before serving.
