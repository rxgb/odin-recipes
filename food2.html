Spaghetti Carbonara

Back to Recipes

Description:

This spaghetti carbonara recipe features a creamy sauce made with eggs, Parmesan cheese, and pancetta. It's a classic Italian dish that's easy to make and always a crowd-pleaser.

Ingredients:

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 100g pancetta, diced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 100g Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Instructions:

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  3. While the spaghetti cooks, heat a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. Add the pancetta and cook until crispy.
  5. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs and Parmesan cheese.
  6. Drain the spaghetti and add it to the skillet with the pancetta.
  7. Remove the skillet from the heat and quickly stir in the egg mixture.
  8. Serve immediately with additional Parmesan cheese and black pepper.
