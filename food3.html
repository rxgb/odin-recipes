Chicken Tikka Masala

Back to Recipes

Description:

This chicken tikka masala recipe features a rich, creamy sauce made with yogurt, tomatoes, and a blend of aromatic spices. It's a delicious and easy-to-make dish that's perfect for a cozy dinner or a special occasion.

Ingredients:

  • 4 chicken breasts, cubed
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  3. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat.
  4. Cook the chicken for 4-5 minutes on each side, or until fully cooked.
  5. In a small saucepan, combine the yogurt, tomatoes, onion, and garlic.
  6. Simmer the sauce for 10-15 minutes, or until it thickens.
  7. Serve the chicken in the creamy sauce.
